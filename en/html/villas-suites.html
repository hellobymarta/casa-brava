Villas & Suites

Philosophy

Stopping is not ceasing to move. It's ceasing to
need to.

Each villa is built from the land, not on top of it: the walls inherit its curve and every opening faces where the morning light falls.

Privacy can't be explained in a brochure. You feel it on a path that only those who open Casa Brava's doors will cross.

The Casa Brava experience

Inside your villa, every detail has already been thought through.

  1. 01

    Rest

    Egyptian cotton bed linen on firm mattresses, and a thick terry bathrobe always waiting in its place.

  2. 02

    Comfort

    Silent air conditioning, high-speed wifi, a safe and a minibar restocked every day without you asking.

  3. 03

    Spaces

    Private pool and terrace, and bath products formulated with plants that grow on the property itself.

  4. 04

    Amenities

    Lombok coffee, tea and infusions, always-cold filtered water, and daily cleaning of the villa.

  5. 05

    Connection

    In-villa massage, private dinner or transfers: just ask, by reservation and at additional cost.
